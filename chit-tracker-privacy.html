Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

In short: Chit Tracker works fully offline. We collect nothing. Your data stays on your phone.

Who we are

Chit Tracker is published by JK National Solution (jkns.co.in). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal or financial information with JK National Solution or anyone else. There is no sign-up, login or account, and the app never asks for your name, phone number or email.

Data you enter

Companies, chits, payments, receipt numbers and notes you enter are stored only in a private database inside the app on your phone. We cannot see or access this data.

Internet access

The app works fully offline and does not request internet permission. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.

App lock

The app uses your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, pattern, PIN or password) to unlock. This check is done by Android. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never receives your fingerprint, face data, pattern or PIN.

Export and backup files

When you choose "Export CSV" or "Back up now", the app creates a file and saves it to a location you pick, such as your phone storage or Google Drive. Once saved, that file is handled by the app or service you chose. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them private.

Deleting your data

You can delete any chit or month inside the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data stored on the phone. Files you exported or backed up must be deleted separately.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
