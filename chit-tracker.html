Why you need it

A chit is simple to join. Hard to keep track of.

Every month a different amount is due, and the company holds whatever extra you pay. A notebook or the company's receipts alone rarely give you the full picture.

"How much have I actually paid so far?"

Twenty months of different amounts, receipts in a drawer and no running total.

"How much advance is the company holding?"

If you pay the full amount every month, the extra builds up. Few people know the exact figure.

"Am I gaining anything at all?"

Without the numbers side by side, you cannot tell if the chit beats a simple bank RD.

Chit Tracker answers all three, every month. Enter what the company asks for and what you pay. The app keeps the running totals, your advance, the free months it covers, and your expected gain, so you are never guessing.

How it works

Three steps. A clear answer every month.

Here is a ₹10,000 × 20 month chit, as the app sees it.

  1. Add your chit

    Pick the company, enter ₹10,000 a month for 20 months and the 5% commission. The app sets up all 20 months.

  2. Record each month

    Enter what the company asks (say ₹8,500 after dividend) and what you paid (₹10,000). The ₹1,500 extra goes to your advance.

  3. See your result

    The app projects the rest: your advance covers the last two months, and you see your final gain straight away.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Chit Tracker free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does it connect to my chit company?

No. Chit Tracker is your personal record. You enter the amounts from your company's receipts, and the app does the calculations. It is not linked to, or run by, any chit company.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Use "Back up now" to save a single backup file to your Google Drive, then "Choose backup file" on the new phone. All your companies, chits and months come across in one step.

If I uninstall the app, is my data lost?

Yes. Because nothing is stored online, the phone holds the only copy. Save a backup to your own Drive once a month so you always have a copy.

How accurate is the projected gain?

Months you have recorded are exact. Future months are estimated from your average so far, or from a worst-case "no dividend" view. The projection updates as each real month is entered.

Can I track chits from more than one company?

Yes. Add as many companies and chits as you like. Chits are grouped by company, with combined monthly totals.
